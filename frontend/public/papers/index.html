Two-Facet Corners for Large Weight-Driven Pipe Organ Reservoirs: Adaptation and Variation

DAVID E. GOGGIN, M.S. · Pre-print

Two-facet corners are designed so that as much of the reservoir corner as feasible is constructed from rigid material, rather than flexible leather or rubber-cloth, to maximize the corner’s durability, maintainability, and precision of pressure regulation. The geometry and parametrical error properties of the two-facet outward-folding corner (first introduced in the context of voided reservoirs) are explored. A newly-discovered two-facet alternate-folding corner type is introduced that has analogous geometric properties to the two-facet outward corner. Banausic fabrication algorithms for the rib fragments of two-facet corners are presented, that facilitate their use in reservoirs large enough that the weight of the rib material would strain traditional cloth and leather inter-rib hinges. A novel type of single-level voided reservoir is described, enabled by the two-facet alternate-folding corner. An imperfect type of camera-folded reservoir, enabled by the commensurable species of two-facet alternate-folding corner is also described. Thought-experiments in designing very large reservoirs are presented.
