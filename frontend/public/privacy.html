Privacy Policy

Signal to Noise Uploader · last updated October 4, 2026

This is a small personal tool I built to upload videos to my own YouTube channel, Signal to Noise. It is not offered to anyone else and has exactly one user: me.

What it accesses

With my permission through Google sign-in, the tool can:

  • upload videos to my YouTube channel,
  • set video thumbnails and schedule publication times,
  • read basic status information about my own videos.

It cannot access anything else in my Google account.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens are stored only on my own computer. No data is sent to any third party, and nothing is collected, shared, or sold.
